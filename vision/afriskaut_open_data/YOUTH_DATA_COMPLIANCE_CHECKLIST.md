# Afriskaut youth data — privacy, consent, data-use and safeguarding checklist

**Version:** 1.0 draft · **Date:** 24 November 2026
**Status:** Working checklist prepared by the engineering side. It is **not legal advice**. Every item marked **OPEN-LEGAL** needs a qualified lawyer (Nigeria and the jurisdiction of the Vel Scout operator) before anyone relies on it.
**Scope:** The Afriskaut Dynasty Scouting League 2024 open data (136 matches, 84,214 events, players aged 16–19) and any later youth-player data from other sources.

## 0. Current rule (unchanged until every gate in §9 is passed)

1. No Afriskaut data is imported into Supabase.
2. No named or otherwise identifiable youth-player profile, report, ranking or score is published or shown to anyone outside the authorised review team.
3. Research code prints aggregate counts only (no names, IDs, teams, per-player values) and uploads no data artifact.
4. A youth-player output is never described as "potential", never ranked for transfer, and never used to contact a player, guardian, club or agent.

## 1. What the open licence does and does not give

| Question | Position |
|---|---|
| What does Apache-2.0 cover? | A copyright and patent licence to use, copy, modify and redistribute the **works** in the publisher's repository, with notice and "as is" terms. |
| Does it say the people in the data agreed to publication or reuse? | **No.** A copyright licence cannot grant consent that the data subjects (players, parents/guardians) did not give. |
| Does it make processing personal data lawful for Vel Scout? | **No.** Lawfulness is a separate question under data-protection law and depends on who we are, where players are, and what we do with the data. |
| Does it give a right to commercial use of the individuals' data or likeness? | **Not by itself.** Whether anything beyond the licence is needed is **OPEN-LEGAL** (Q1, Q2). |
| Can we rely on the publisher having done the legal work? | **No, not without written evidence** (§3). A public release is not proof of valid consent or a lawful basis. |
| Trademarks and names | Apache-2.0 does not grant trademark rights. Do not imply endorsement by Afriskaut, the league or any club. |

## 3. Provenance and consent — questions for the publisher (in writing)

Ask Afriskaut for written answers. Keep the replies and dates in `vision/afriskaut_open_data/` (without personal data). Do **not** contact players, guardians or clubs directly.

1. Who is the data controller for the underlying personal data, and in which country?
2. How was the data collected (broadcast, own cameras, league feed) and under whose permission (league, clubs)?
3. Were players and, for minors, parents/guardians informed and was consent obtained? For what purposes and for how long? Can they provide the consent text?
4. Does the consent cover **public release**, **reuse by third parties** and **commercial use**?
5. Are the player IDs stable pseudonyms generated by the publisher? Is a key table held by the publisher?
6. What is the process for a player or guardian to ask for removal or correction, and how are those requests propagated to downstream users?
7. Is there a data-protection assessment or legal opinion for the release?
8. What uses does the publisher expressly allow or forbid beyond the licence text (scouting, rankings, transfer decisions, commercial products)?

**Rule:** if answers 3–4 are missing or negative, no identifiable youth data is used beyond aggregate research.

## 5. Safeguarding

1. **No contact:** nothing in the product contacts, tags, messages or identifies a minor, guardian, school or club.
2. **No location or schedule exposure:** never publish where a minor trains, studies, lives or will play next.
3. **No images or video of minors** are processed or stored from this source.
4. **No public youth rankings** or comparative league tables of named minors.
5. **Protected-status language:** any youth-related report states that it describes observed performance in sampled matches only, not potential, and gives no transfer or contract advice.
6. **Human review:** a named responsible person (to be assigned) approves any internal use of youth data and records the decision.
7. **Concern route:** a documented way to report a safeguarding concern about the data or the product (owner to assign a contact).
8. **Staff access:** only authenticated Vel staff with a role; youth data, if ever stored, has its own tables/policies and is excluded from every anonymous/public path.

## 6. Technical controls required before any storage

- **Minimisation:** keep only fields needed for the approved purpose; drop names, dates of birth and anything not required.
- **Separate store:** a separate schema (or project) with RLS on, no `anon` access, column grants limited to staff, and no inclusion in any public view or report renderer.
- **Pseudonymisation:** if IDs are stored, use our own keyed identifiers; keep the mapping outside the analytics tables with restricted access.
- **Retention and deletion:** a documented retention period and a tested procedure to delete one player across tables, logs, backups and derived reports.
- **Provenance fields:** every row carries source, licence, retrieval date, and the consent status recorded in §3.
- **Audit:** access to youth tables is logged; the live schema is checked with the Supabase security advisor after every change.
- **Rollback:** any migration for this data is tested in a rolled-back transaction first and has a documented rollback.
- **Public site:** `index.html` and the static player directory contain no youth data; a CI check should fail if youth identifiers appear in published files (**TODO**).

## 7. Product restrictions for youth data

Not allowed until the gates in §9 are passed and recorded: public youth profiles; any score, grade or rank of a named minor; "potential", "prospect" or transfer language; commercial use or resale; combining with other sources to identify a person; sharing outside the review team.

Allowed now: aggregate, non-identifying research that tests our parsers and metric definitions (current practice).

## 8. Evidence to keep

For each step: date, who decided, what was decided, and the document or message that supports it. Store counsel's written opinion and the publisher's replies as references (no personal data in the repository).

## 9. Gates

| Gate | What must be true | Status |
|---|---|---|
| G0 — Research only | Aggregate output only, no storage, no identifiers in logs. | **Met** (current practice) |

| G2 — Provenance | Publisher answers to §3 received and filed. | Not done |
| G3 — Legal opinion | Q1–Q8 answered in writing by counsel. | Not done |
| G4 — DPIA and controls | Impact assessment completed; §6 controls built and tested in rolled-back tests. | Not done |
| G5 — Owner approval | Named owner signs off the exact use (internal only / limited pilot). | Not done |
| G6 — Public or commercial use | Separate sign-off beyond G5; likely needs consent evidence. | Not started |

No youth data enters Supabase before G5 is recorded.

# Vel Scouting — Proje Yol Haritası v1.0

**Tarih:** 10 Kasım 2026  
**Durum:** Metodoloji temeli oluşturuldu. Güncel ürün yönü: özel scouting video/maç kaynak sistemi ile kamuya açık raporlama alanını kesin biçimde ayırmak.

## 1. Proje hedefi ve ürün ilkeleri

Vel Scouting; farklı yaş ve seviyelerdeki futbolcuları kapsayan, özellikle genç yeteneklere odaklanan bağımsız bir futbolcu değerlendirme platformudur.

İlk ürünün amacı futbolcuyu yalnızca “potansiyelli” diye etiketlemek değil; önce bugünkü oyununu, oynadığı rolü ve gözlemlenebilir performansını kanıta dayalı biçimde tanımlamaktır. Potansiyel ve transfer uygunluğu daha sonra, ayrı değerlendirme katmanları olarak ele alınacaktır.

Temel ilkeler:
- Gözlem ve kanıt, kanaatin önünde gelir.
- Maç bağlamı, oyuncunun rolü ve takımın oyun yapısı puanlamaya dâhil edilir.
- Mevcut performans, potansiyel ve transfer uygunluğu birbirine karıştırılmaz.
- Yetersiz video veya veri, düşük puan olarak yorumlanmaz.
- Metodoloji sürümü ve rapor geçmişi korunur.
- Genç oyuncuların mahremiyeti ve güvenliği ürün tasarımının başından itibaren gözetilir.
- Video içerikleri izinsiz kopyalanmaz veya yeniden yayımlanmaz; kaynak ve kullanım hakları kaydedilir.

## 2. Şu ana kadar tamamlananlar

- Ortak değerlendirme çerçevesi v1.0 oluşturuldu.
- Stoper, bek/kanat bek, ön libero/merkez orta saha, hücumcu orta saha/10 numara, kanat, santrfor ve kaleci metodoloji dokümanları hazırlandı.
- Pozisyon ve rol sınırları dokümante edildi.
- İlk metodoloji kalite denetimi tamamlandı: ağırlık toplamları, alan hesapları ve rol ayrımları kontrol edildi.
- Stoper için 37 alt kriterin tamamına gözlenebilir davranış tanımları ve altı puan bandı içeren puanlama çıpaları v1.0 hazırlandı.
- Kalite denetimi raporunda puanlama çıpalarının yalnızca stoper için tamamlandığı, diğer altı pozisyon grubunun ve pilot kalibrasyonun beklediği açıkça kaydedildi.

**Önemli:** Bunlar dokümantasyon ve mantıksal tutarlılık kontrolleridir. Henüz gerçek maç üzerinde pilot yapılmış veya iki değerlendirici arasında kalibrasyon kanıtlanmış değildir.

## 7. Continuation audit — 11 November 2026

### Confirmed live database access posture

A read-only audit of the live Supabase project confirmed:
- All 19 public tables have RLS enabled.
- Anonymous table-level SELECT is granted only for `players`, `clubs`, and `scout_reports`; their policies expose published player profiles, clubs linked to published players, and published reports belonging to published players.
- The remaining 16 tables do not grant anonymous SELECT. The `private` schema does not grant USAGE to `anon`.
- `private.is_vel_staff()` and `private.is_vel_admin()` are SECURITY DEFINER helpers with an empty search_path and EXECUTE granted only to `authenticated` (plus the database owner). They derive authorization from `auth.uid()` and `public.user_roles`.
- `create_scout_report_bundle()` and `create_multi_match_scout_report_bundle()` are SECURITY INVOKER functions with an empty search_path; EXECUTE is granted to `authenticated`, not `anon`.

These are SQL/catalog-level checks. They do not replace a browser Data API test with real anonymous, staff, and non-staff sessions.

### Admin browser diagnostics expanded

On 11 November 2026, `admin.html` was updated so its read-only anonymous-access diagnostic checks all 16 private tables, rather than the previous subset of 10. The change was committed to `main`:

### Admin report workflow integration

The admin report workflow was updated on 11 November 2026 and now uses the existing secure report-bundle RPCs instead of inserting into `scout_reports` and `report_versions` separately:
- One-match / same-match session bundles call `create_scout_report_bundle()`.
- Multi-match bundles call `create_multi_match_scout_report_bundle()`; the UI and database both enforce the completed/locked session requirement and the multi-match minimum.
- Methodology version is read from the selected `rubric_versions` row; the hardcoded `0.8` value was removed.
- The report snapshot captures the selected player, rubric, session/match context, criterion scores, timestamped observations, and linked video-source metadata.

## 27. Database-level immutability for completed/locked scouting sessions (21 November 2026)

### Finding

The completed/locked session lock existed only in `admin.html`. ## 28. Afriskaut match clocks and conservative player minutes (22 November 2026)

### What was checked (real data, CI runner, aggregate counts only)

All checks ran on the public Afriskaut Dynasty Scouting League 2024 sample (136 matches, 84,214 events) inside GitHub Actions. They print counts only: no player names, IDs, team names or per-player values, nothing is written to disk and Supabase is not touched.

- **Structure (PR #66):** each match has a starting eleven, a bench and a list of substitutions (`in`/`out`) that carries no minute. `match.json` has `match_start_time`, `first_half_end_time`, `second_half_start_time` and `match_end_time` as HH:MM:SS; extra-time and penalty fields are empty in all 136 matches. Events have `event_half` (0-2) and `event_start_time`/`event_stop_time` (HH:MM:SS) but no separate period field.
- **Clocks (PR #67):** all four match clock fields are present and ordered in 136/136 matches. 99.7% of `event_half = 1` events fall in the first-half window and 99.9% of `event_half = 2` events in the second-half window; the 91 events with `event_half = 0` are scattered and are not used. Regular-time played length is 46.3-91.9 minutes (median 81.4, halves of about 40 minutes), so 90 minutes is never assumed.
- **Substitutions (PR #67):** 796 `SUBSTITUTION` events against 572 listed substitutions. Event and list counts agree in only 57 matches (53 with more events, 26 with fewer). 492 events belong to the player going off, 8 to the player coming on, 291 to players not listed in the substitution list. Cards are recorded as `event_name = CARD` (event_type `FOUL` or `DISSENT`, outcome `YELLOW` or `RED`, where RED is a sending off in the publisher's event map): 228 card events, of which 15 are red (9 `FOUL`, 6 `DISSENT`). An earlier version of this section wrongly stated that the feed has no card events; it was corrected on 23 November 2026 after reading the publisher's event map.

### Minutes calculation (PR #68)

`vision/afriskaut_open_data/player_minutes.py` returns minutes only when the match clock, squad lists, listed substitutions and `SUBSTITUTION` events reconcile; the half-time break is excluded. Everything else is `None` with a reason.

- `verified`: starter substituted off, substitute coming on, or a player sent off by a single `RED` card event, each with a single consistent time.
- `derived_no_exit_recorded`: starter with no recorded exit. Never `verified`, because an injury without replacement cannot be detected. It is kept only in matches whose substitution records are fully consistent.
- `not_used_listed_bench`: listed bench player with no events (0 minutes).
- `unavailable`: `None` plus an explicit reason.

Result on the real sample (after red-card handling): substitution records are fully consistent in only 22 of 136 matches, so in the other 114 matches an unrecorded exit cannot be excluded. Player-match records: 893 `verified` (including 11 starters and 1 substitute ended by a red card), 214 `derived_no_exit_recorded`, 923 `not_used_listed_bench`, 2,208 `unavailable` (1,412 of them are starters withheld for incomplete match records). 1,039 of the 3,169 players with events (32.8%) have minutes. Ten players with two `YELLOW` cards and no `RED` event are withheld because a second yellow normally means a sending off but is not always coded as one.

Self-consistency check: a player whose own events fall outside the computed interval is withheld. At 10 s tolerance this affected 69 players (73 at 0 s, 64 at 60 s), so the result does not depend on clock noise; it points to inconsistent source records.

### Evidence and limits

- 75 unit tests pass locally and in CI (synthetic fixtures: half-time break, substitution times, missing or conflicting events, unlisted events, ambiguous players, unusable clocks); the real-data validator ran in CI on every push to the PR.
- Per-90 values are intentionally not produced yet. If used later, per-90 must use `verified` and `derived_no_exit_recorded` minutes only and must show the status.
- Not yet done: comparing the publisher's event-map definitions with the actual event labels, an anonymous role-metric sample with verified minutes only, and the privacy, consent and safeguarding review. No Afriskaut data was imported into Supabase and no named youth profile exists or is public.


## 29. Afriskaut centre-back metrics and the decision to pause scoring use (23 November 2026)

- **PR #72:** `vision/afriskaut_open_data/cb_profile.py` defines raw centre-back metrics by (`event_name`, `event_type`, `event_outcome`) following the publisher's event map, so FOUL card rows and throw-in/corner SHORT/LONG rows are not mixed into fouls and open-play passes. Outcomes are reported as "labelled successful"; no pass-completion percentage is produced because KEY_PASS/ASSIST semantics are undocumented. Per-90 needs `verified` or consistent-match `derived` minutes and at least 45 minutes; quartiles only for cohorts of 10 or more. 87 unit tests pass.
- **Real-data result (CI, aggregate only):** of 352 lineup centre-back player-matches, 312 have no usable minutes (substitution records are consistent in only 22 of 136 matches); 34 qualify for per-90. Medians for many metrics are 0 in that cohort.
- **Density check (PR #73):** event attribution is not the cause of the low counts (all 84,214 events have a player_id, 84,193 match the same-match squad). The sample simply holds about 275 PASS rows per match (median 16.6 per starter), far fewer than a real match, so it appears to be a coded subset rather than a full pass log. Centre-back PASS rows per lineup player-match average about 14 overall but about 5 in the 34 qualifying player-matches, which suggests matches with reliable minutes may also be sparser coded.
- **Decision:** Afriskaut stays a raw-count research source. It is not used for scores, rankings or any player-facing number, and its volume metrics are not interpreted as performance. Extending the same definitions to other positions is deferred. Work moves to the end-to-end staff scouting flow (session, observations, report). Privacy, consent and safeguarding review remains required before any Supabase import or named youth profile.

**Player erasure (checklist Q7), technical picture.** Foreign keys from a player: `player_matches` and `scout_reports` cascade; `scouting_sessions → player_matches` is RESTRICT, and sessions are protected by design (completed/locked sessions cannot be deleted; their scores, observations and video links have session-lock guards; reports' session links are RESTRICT). Options for the owner to decide once Q7 is answered: (a) hard erasure through an admin-only function that sets a transaction-local flag honoured by the guards, deletes reports, sessions and the player in one transaction and writes a data-less audit row; (b) anonymisation (blank name/birth date/nationality, redact free text, keep the evidence structure) which keeps methodology history but may not satisfy a deletion request. Neither is built: it touches six guards and needs the legal answer first. The live database currently holds 1 player, 1 session and 0 reports.
